Swiss Alps - Top 12 Places

Peaks • Glaciers • Scenic Trails

Matterhorn

The iconic pyramid-shaped peak on the border of Switzerland and Italy.

Eiger

Famous for the North Face climbing routes and alpine scenery.

Jungfrau

Part of the “Top of Europe” region with breathtaking glacier views.

Schilthorn

Famous for panoramic views and the Piz Gloria revolving restaurant.

Grindelwald

A picturesque village at the foot of the Eiger with alpine trails.

Zermatt

Charming mountain village, base for Matterhorn adventures.

Mont Blanc Region

Snow-capped peaks and world-class skiing.

Lauterbrunnen Valley

Deep valley with stunning waterfalls and alpine meadows.

Lake Oeschinen

Alpine lake with crystal waters, perfect for hiking and boating.

Rosenlaui Glacier

Scenic glacier area with trails and waterfalls.

Mount Titlis

Famous for its ice cave and cable car views over the Alps.

Mount Rigi

Known as the “Queen of the Mountains,” offering panoramic views.
