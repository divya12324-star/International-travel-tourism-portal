🌴 Top 12 Famous Places in Bora Bora

Mount Otemanu

The iconic volcanic peak offering breathtaking views of Bora Bora.

Matira Beach

World-famous white sand beach with shallow crystal-clear waters.

Lagoonarium

A natural aquarium where visitors can swim with marine life.

Coral Gardens

Popular snorkeling spot with vibrant corals and tropical fish.

Mount Pahia

Second-highest peak ideal for adventurous hiking experiences.

Vaitape Village

Main town of Bora Bora known for shops and local culture.

Tupitipiti Point

Scenic viewpoint offering panoramic lagoon and island views.

Le Meridien Bora Bora

Luxury resort famous for overwater bungalows.

Sofitel Private Island

Exclusive island resort offering unmatched privacy.

Faanui Bay

Historic bay with stunning mountain and lagoon scenery.

Lagoon Cruise

Boat tour around Bora Bora’s turquoise lagoon.

Sunset Point

Perfect spot to witness romantic sunsets over the lagoon.
