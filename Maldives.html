🌊 Top Tourist Places in Maldives

Vaadhoo Island

Famous for the glowing Sea of Stars caused by bioluminescent plankton.

Banana Reef

World-famous snorkeling and diving site with vibrant coral reefs.

Maafushi Island

Budget-friendly island offering water sports and local culture.

Baa Atoll

UNESCO Biosphere Reserve famous for manta rays and whale sharks.

Hulhumalé Beach

Calm beach near Malé, ideal for sunsets and peaceful walks.

Malé City

The vibrant capital city with mosques, markets, and culture.

Thoondu Beach

Unique pebble beach with crystal-clear shallow waters.

Meeru Island

Popular resort island with palm-lined beaches and lagoons.

Sun Island

Luxury island resort known for long beaches and overwater villas.

Alimatha Island

Famous for night diving and clear turquoise lagoons.

Fihalhohi Island

Peaceful resort island surrounded by coral reefs.

Ari Atoll

Best place to spot whale sharks and enjoy diving adventures.
