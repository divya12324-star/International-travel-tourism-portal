Mount Rainier - Top 12 Places

Explore • Hike • Discover the Beauty

Paradise Area

Popular viewpoint with wildflower meadows and scenic trails.

Sunrise Visitor Center

Highest point accessible by car, offering stunning sunrise views.

Grove of the Patriarchs

A serene forest trail with ancient towering trees.

Nisqually Vista

Easy trails with panoramic views of glaciers and valleys.

Mowich Lake

Beautiful alpine lake, great for camping and kayaking.

Christine Falls

Photogenic waterfall easily accessible from the main road.

Tipsoo Lake

Scenic lake reflecting Mount Rainier’s peak, perfect for photos.

Reflection Lakes

Popular spot for capturing mirrored mountain views in calm waters.

Tahoma Creek

Peaceful creek with hiking trails along the river.

Sunset at Mount Rainier

Best viewpoint to enjoy breathtaking sunsets over the mountain.

Comet Falls

One of the tallest waterfalls in the park, surrounded by lush forest.

Skyline Trail

Challenging hiking trail offering views of glaciers, valleys, and wildflowers.
